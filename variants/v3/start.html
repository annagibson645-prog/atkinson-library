Where to start

Atkinson wrote a great deal. Pick the line that sounds like you and follow its roadmap: the books in order, with a reason for each step, and a checklist that remembers your progress.

  • The core idea

    You want his central teaching first

    Four books that build the main argument: thought is a force, like attracts like, and it can be directed.

    4 books, about 15.3 hr

    See the roadmap

  • Attention, memory and clear thinking

    You want to train your own mind

    From concentration to memory to reasoning: the working skills the rest of his system depends on.

    5 books, about 15.9 hr

    See the roadmap

  • Breath, body and the yogi lessons

    You are curious about yoga and the East

    The Yogi Ramacharaka course in a sensible order, starting with the breath.

    6 books, about 29.1 hr

    See the roadmap

  • Influence, magnetism and persuasion

    You want to understand how people affect each other

    How attention, will and presence work between people, and how that applies in business.

    6 books, about 15.5 hr

    See the roadmap

  • Mind, body and healing

    You want his writing on health and the mind

    Early twentieth century writing on mental and physical healing, from self-suggestion to water and breath.

    7 books, about 15.1 hr

    See the roadmap

  • The psychic side

    You want the strange material

    Telepathy, clairvoyance, crystal gazing, auras and mediumship, from experiments to theory.

    6 books, about 16.3 hr

    See the roadmap

  • The inner mind

    You want to understand the subconscious

    From everyday psychology down through the subconscious and up to the superconscious.

    5 books, about 13.3 hr

    See the roadmap

  • Philosophy and religion

    You read for the ideas behind the practice

    Where New Thought came from, and the Christian, Indian and reincarnation teachings he wrote about.

    8 books, about 24.8 hr

    See the roadmap

  • The hidden teachings

    You want the Hermetic and Rosicrucian material

    The Arcane Teachings, the formulas for mental alchemy, and the Rosicrucian doctrines.

    4 books, about 12.5 hr

    See the roadmap

  • The twelve-volume course

    You want one whole system, in order

    Personal Power runs from the master self to regeneration. Read it as written, one volume at a time.

    12 books, about 31.4 hr

    See the roadmap

  • Short on time

    You have an evening, not a month

    The six shortest books in the library. Each can be finished in one sitting.

    6 books, about 5.8 hr

    See the roadmap

The books of William Walker Atkinson (1862–1932), in the public domain. Free to read, with no signup. Introductions and notes on this site are written for the library; the book text is Atkinson’s own.
