William Walker Atkinson

1862–1932. A lawyer turned New Thought editor and one of the most prolific American writers on mind, will and the Eastern traditions.

Atkinson was born in Baltimore and trained as a lawyer. After a breakdown in health and fortune in the 1890s he turned to the mental-healing and New Thought movement, edited the magazines Suggestion and New Thought, and then wrote at remarkable speed for the next thirty years.

Many names

He published under his own name and under several others. The best known are Yogi Ramacharaka (the yoga and Eastern philosophy lessons), Theron Q. Dumont (personal magnetism, concentration, mental influence) and Swami Panchadasi (clairvoyance, the aura, mediumship). Most of the books here appeared first through the Yogi Publication Society in Chicago.

What is not settled

Some attributions are debated. The Kybalion, credited to “Three Initiates”, is often assigned to him but is not part of this collection. The Personal Power volumes are included in the collected works this library was built from; if you are researching attribution, check them against a bibliography.

Why there are no lectures

Atkinson wrote for the page. No recordings of him are known, so this library is text only. Every book is split into chapters you can read in order, and every word is searchable.

Copyright

Atkinson died in 1932, and the books here were published in the early twentieth century. They are in the public domain in the United States. The text may contain small errors from digitisation.
