Influence, magnetism and persuasion

How attention, will and presence work between people, and how that applies in business.

6 books, about 15.5 hr of reading. At 30 minutes a day that is about 31 days; at a book a week, 6 weeks.

0 of 6 finished

  1. Step 1

    Mental Fascination

    21 chapters, about 3.9 hr

    Fascination and attraction between minds, with experiments.

    Read it 1 technique to try

  2. Step 2

    Practical Mental Influence

    12 chapters, about 85 min

    A short, practical treatment of mental influence.

    Read it

  3. Step 3

    The Art and Science of Personal Magnetism

    20 chapters, about 2.6 hr

    Personal magnetism, with nerve-force and gaze exercises.

    Read it 2 techniques to try

  4. Step 4

    How to Read Human Nature

    19 chapters, about 2.1 hr

    Reading character from inner states and outer forms.

    Read it

  5. Step 5

    The Psychology of Salesmanship

    10 chapters, about 3.2 hr

    Putting influence to work in selling.

    Read it

  6. Step 6

    Thought-Force in Business and Everyday Life

    16 chapters, about 2.3 hr

    Thought-force applied to work and daily life.

    Read it 3 techniques to try
