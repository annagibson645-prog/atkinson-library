Trails

Every roadmap is a trail of books to read in order, with a ring around each book that fills as you read its chapters. Choose a level, or walk the whole way.

BeginnerIntermediateAdvanced

  • The whole way: beginner to advanced

    You want one path through the whole library

    Fourteen books across three stages. Each stage builds on the one before it.

    14 books, about 49.5 hr

    Open the trail

Beginner

No background needed. Shorter, plainer books that set out the main ideas.

  • Short on time

    You have an evening, not a month

    The six shortest books in the library. Each takes about an hour and a half or less.

    6 books, about 5.8 hr

    Open the trail

  • The core idea

    You want his central teaching first

    Four books that build the main argument: thought is a force, like attracts like, and it can be directed.

    6 books, about 14.8 hr

    Open the trail

  • Attention, memory and clear thinking

    You want to train your own mind

    From concentration to memory to reasoning: the working skills the rest of his system depends on.

    7 books, about 21.1 hr

    Open the trail

Intermediate

You know the core idea and want to go deeper in one direction.

  • The inner mind

    You want to understand the subconscious

    From everyday psychology down into the subconscious, with suggestion as the working tool.

    4 books, about 10.2 hr

    Read first: The core idea

    Open the trail

  • Yoga: breath and body

    You are curious about yoga and the East

    The first half of the Yogi Ramacharaka course: breath, body and the mental lessons.

    3 books, about 11.6 hr

    Read first: The core idea

    Open the trail

  • Influence, magnetism and persuasion

    You want to understand how people affect each other

    How attention, will and presence work between people, and how that applies in business.

    6 books, about 15.5 hr

    Read first: Attention, memory and clear thinking

    Open the trail

  • Mind, body and healing

    You want his writing on health and the mind

    Early twentieth century writing on mental and physical healing, from self-suggestion to water and breath.

    6 books, about 12.1 hr

    Read first: The core idea

    Open the trail

  • The psychic side

    You want the strange material

    Telepathy, clairvoyance, crystal gazing, auras and mediumship, from experiments to theory.

    6 books, about 16.3 hr

    Read first: Attention, memory and clear thinking

    Open the trail

  • Personal Power I: the self

    You want one whole system, in order

    Volumes I to IV of the twelve-volume course: the master self, imagination, desire and faith.

    4 books, about 10.4 hr

    Read first: The core idea

    Open the trail

  • Personal Power II: the powers of mind

    You finished Personal Power I

    Volumes V to VIII: will, the subconscious, spirit and thought.

    4 books, about 10.6 hr

    Read first: Personal Power I: the self

    Open the trail

Advanced

The long courses, the philosophy and the hidden teachings.

  • Yoga: the philosophy

    You finished the breath and body trail

    The second half of the Yogi Ramacharaka course: the philosophy, the wisdom path and the advanced lessons.

    3 books, about 17.5 hr

    Read first: Yoga: breath and body

    Open the trail

  • Philosophy and religion

    You read for the ideas behind the practice

    Where New Thought came from, and the Christian, Indian and reincarnation teachings he wrote about.

    8 books, about 24.8 hr

    Read first: The core idea

    Open the trail

  • Personal Power III: mastery

    You finished Personal Power II

    Volumes IX to XII: perception, reasoning, character and regeneration.

    4 books, about 10.4 hr

    Read first: Personal Power II: the powers of mind

    Open the trail

  • Mental magic

    You want the long, full statement of the method

    The two largest treatments of the New Thought method: Mind Power and the seven-lesson course.

    2 books, about 13.4 hr

    Read first: The core idea

    Open the trail

  • The hidden teachings

    You want the Hermetic and Rosicrucian material

    The Arcane Teachings, the formulas for mental alchemy, and the Rosicrucian doctrines.

    4 books, about 12.4 hr

    Read first: The inner mind
