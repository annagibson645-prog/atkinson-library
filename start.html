Where to start

Atkinson wrote a great deal. Pick the line that sounds like you.

  • You want the core idea

    A compact statement of the law of attraction in the thought world. Read it first, then move to The Law of the New Thought for the wider frame.

    Read Thought Vibration 1.6 hr

  • You want something to practise

    A course on training attention, which the rest of his mental work depends on.

    Read The Power of Concentration 2.7 hr

  • You are curious about yoga and the East

    A complete manual of Oriental breathing philosophy. Then try Fourteen Lessons in Yogi Philosophy.

    Read The Hindu-Yogi Science of Breath 1.6 hr

  • You want the strange material

    Published under the Swami Panchadasi name. Pair it with The Human Aura and Practical Mind-Reading.

    Read Clairvoyance and Occult Powers 5.6 hr

  • You want a whole system

    Twelve volumes, from the master self to regeneration. Volume I is the place to begin.

    Read Personal Power 2.4 hr

The books of William Walker Atkinson (1862–1932), in the public domain. Free to read, with no signup. Introductions and notes on this site are written for the library; the book text is Atkinson’s own.
